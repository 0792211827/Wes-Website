title: Background
---

I studied statistics at Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture and Technology
from 2019, graduating in December 2023.

My first applied work came during the degree. In early 2023 I interned as a data
analyst at **NITA**, building and deploying machine learning models to support
operational decisions, integrating external data APIs to automate ingestion, and
writing up the analysis so stakeholders could act on it without needing me to
interpret it. In mid-2024 I took a remote data science internship with
**Forage**, working on predictive models for customer booking behaviour and
sentiment analysis over customer reviews, alongside the preprocessing workflows
that fed both.

Two habits came out of that. Putting everything under version control, so a
result could be reproduced rather than recalled — and spending more time on the
data pipeline than on the model, because that is usually where the errors
actually are.

Alongside the applied work I've worked through formal training on the deployment
side of the field: MLOps tooling with MLflow and Hugging Face, and advanced
learning algorithms, both via Coursera, plus Python and data science coursework
through DataCamp.
