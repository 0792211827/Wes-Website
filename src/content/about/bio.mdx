I'm Wesley Nyamosi, a data scientist and AI engineer based in Kenya. I work on
predictive modelling, natural language processing, and the unglamorous
engineering that sits between a trained model and a system people can rely on.

My background is statistics — a BSc from Jomo Kenyatta University of Agriculture
and Technology — which shapes how I approach machine learning. I care about what
the data can actually support: whether a result is distinguishable from noise,
whether a baseline was beaten, and whether the evaluation reflects how the model
will be used rather than how it was trained.
